job-agent privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026.

job-agent is a personal, single-user tool. Only its owner signs in to it, and it isn't offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, job-agent:

  • reads job-alert emails, and the owner's replies to its daily email, in the owner's Gmail account
  • sends one email a day, to the owner's own address only

It never sends email to anyone else, and never changes or deletes email.

How the data is used

Job titles, companies and job descriptions are stored in the owner's private GitHub repository. They're sent to the Anthropic API so the jobs can be ranked against the owner's CV. Nothing is sold, used for advertising or shared with anyone else.

Google user data

job-agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact
